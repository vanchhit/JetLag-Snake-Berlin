# BERLIN SNAKE — CARD CONTENT
*The complete deck. Print-friendly card layout in `cards.html`. The web app pulls from this canonical list.*

## CHALLENGE CARDS (48)

### EASY — 1 point each (24 cards)

1. **STATION SELFIE** — Photograph yourself with the station name sign clearly visible behind you.
2. **THE U-BAHN PORTRAIT** — Photograph the architectural detail of the station that you find most distinctive (a tile, a column, a mosaic).
3. **THE SPÄTI RUN** — Walk to the nearest Späti. Photograph the storefront. Buy nothing — just observe.
4. **THE LINE GUARDIAN** — Photograph the line color stripe on the wall or floor with at least two line designations visible (e.g., U2, S5).
5. **THE LIST** — Note the next 3 outbound destinations shown on the platform display. Type them into the app.
6. **THE EXIT** — Leave via a different exit than you entered. Photograph the street sign of the road you emerge onto.
7. **THE WAIT** — Watch one train arrive and depart without boarding. Photograph it as it leaves.
8. **THE TICKET** — Photograph the BVG ticket vending machine at this station.
9. **THE SCHEDULE** — Find the printed timetable for any line and photograph it.
10. **THE BIN** — Find the nearest public trash bin outside the station. Photograph it. (Yes, really.)
11. **THE BIKE** — Photograph a Berlin-style cargo bike or three bikes locked together.
12. **THE GRAFFITI** — Photograph a piece of graffiti or street art within 100m of the station.
13. **THE TREE** — Find a tree within 50m of the station entrance. Photograph it with the station entrance in the same frame.
14. **THE DOORBELL** — Photograph a building's intercom panel (Klingelschild). Bonus points if it has 20+ buttons.
15. **THE MAILBOX** — Find a yellow Deutsche Post mailbox. Photograph it.
16. **THE BIER** — Photograph a beer advertisement (Berliner, Schultheiss, Augustiner, etc.) on any surface.
17. **THE WINDOW** — Photograph an interesting window — laundry hanging, plants, a cat, an old sign.
18. **THE WALK SIGN** — Photograph the Ampelmann (East Berlin walking-figure pedestrian signal).
19. **THE WURST** — Find any establishment selling currywurst within 200m. Photograph the sign.
20. **THE CHURCH** — Locate any church tower or spire visible from the station. Photograph it.
21. **THE NUMBER** — Find a house with the number 42 within sight of the station. Photograph it.
22. **THE LANGUAGE** — Photograph a sign in a language other than German or English.
23. **THE TIME** — Photograph any visible public clock and note the time in the app.
24. **THE COLOR** — Photograph three things outside the station that match the line color of this station (e.g., for U2: red things).

### MEDIUM — 2 points each (16 cards)

25. **THE STRANGER'S TIP** — Ask one stranger near the station for a restaurant recommendation in the neighborhood. Photograph the place's exterior. (You don't have to eat there.)
26. **THE FIVE-MINUTE BENCH** — Find a public bench within 300m. Sit on it for a full 5 minutes. Photograph the view from your sit.
27. **THE STOLPERSTEIN** — Locate a Stolperstein (brass cobblestone memorial). Photograph it. (Nearly every Berlin street has at least one.)
28. **THE MURAL** — Find a piece of street art at least 2 meters tall. Photograph it fully.
29. **THE SOUVENIR HUNT** — Find a souvenir shop or kiosk selling Berlin-branded items. Photograph the most absurd souvenir you can spot.
30. **THE WATER** — Find any body of water — fountain, canal, puddle, river — within 400m. Photograph it.
31. **THE CROSSING** — Cross the largest road near the station and photograph the station from the other side.
32. **THE ORDER** — Order a beverage from the nearest café in German. Photograph the cup or receipt.
33. **THE BUILDING NUMBER** — Photograph a house number that matches the current station's U/S line number (e.g., at a U7 station, find house number 7).
34. **THE TRANSIT MAP** — Find a paper U/S-Bahn map (often in stations or in vehicles). Photograph it.
35. **THE BOOK** — Find a bookshop, library, or one of Berlin's outdoor "Bücherschrank" mini-libraries. Photograph the books.
36. **THE BIKE LANE** — Stand on a bike lane (carefully). Photograph your feet on it. Survive.
37. **THE PIGEON** — Photograph a single pigeon doing anything interesting (eating, fighting, posing).
38. **THE BIG SIGN** — Find a neon sign or large illuminated sign within 300m. Photograph it.
39. **THE BAKERY** — Find a Bäckerei. Photograph the pastry display.
40. **THE PARK ENTRY** — Find the nearest park or green space entrance. Photograph the entrance sign or gate.

### HARD — 3 points each (8 cards)

41. **THE FIVE MINUTES OUT** — Walk in any direction for 5 minutes. Photograph where you ended up with a recognizable landmark in frame. Walk back.
42. **THE STRANGER'S STORY** — Find someone willing to tell you one sentence about why they like or dislike this neighborhood. With consent, audio-record or note their answer in the app. No photos of them.
43. **THE LOCAL FOOD** — Buy and eat one item that costs under €4 from a vendor within 5 minutes of the station. Photograph it before eating.
44. **THE GHOST STATION** — Without using your phone for navigation, find your way back to the station from 200m away. Photograph yourself at the entrance.
45. **THE LANGUAGE EXCHANGE** — Compliment someone on something in German (their dog, jacket, bike). Note what you said in the app.
46. **THE COMPLETE LOOP** — Walk around the block. Photograph the station entrance from each of the four sides (or as many as exist).
47. **THE COLD WAR CHECK** — Find a piece of physical evidence of the divided city — a Wall remnant, a memorial, an East/West architectural contrast. Photograph it.
48. **THE NAME GAME** — Research the person/place the station is named after using only signs in the station. Type a one-sentence summary into the app.

## CURSE CARDS (20)

1. **CURSE OF THE BEAR** — Your next 3 claims must each be on a different U-Bahn line.
2. **CURSE OF THE CURRYWURST** — Eat a snack at your next station before claiming it (+15 min minimum).
3. **CURSE OF THE TOURIST** — Take a selfie with a (consenting) stranger at each of your next 2 stations.
4. **CURSE OF THE SLOWPOKE** — No S-Bahn for 30 minutes.
5. **CURSE OF THE WALL** — Your next claim must be on the East side of the former Wall. (Hunter chooses East or West.)
6. **CURSE OF THE LOOP** — Your next claim must be on the Ringbahn (S41/S42).
7. **CURSE OF THE ECHO** — For 60 minutes, your location pings fire every 20 minutes instead of 30.
8. **CURSE OF THE STAIRMASTER** — At your next station, exit and re-enter via the stairs. No escalators. Photograph the stairs.
9. **CURSE OF THE POLITE GUEST** — Your next 2 challenges must be completed in conversation with at least one stranger.
10. **CURSE OF THE COIN** — Flip a coin in the app. Heads: your next claim must be North of you. Tails: South.
11. **CURSE OF THE LONG WAY** — Skip the next direct adjacent station. Your next claim must be 2+ stops away (no Leap Power consumed; this replaces it for one move).
12. **CURSE OF THE STREETCAR** — One of your next 3 claims must include a tram (M-line) photo. If you haven't taken one, the claim doesn't count.
13. **CURSE OF THE SILENT TYPE** — For 30 minutes, no using your phone for routing. Paper map or memory only.
14. **CURSE OF THE EXPENSIVE TASTE** — Buy something costing more than €5 at your next station's claim.
15. **CURSE OF THE PHOTOGRAPHER** — Your next claim's challenge proof must be a video, not a photo.
16. **CURSE OF THE GHOST** — Your next claim must be a station that was once a "Geisterbahnhof" (ghost station of the divided city). Use signs/research in the app.
17. **CURSE OF THE EARLY BIRD** — Sing one verse of any song aloud at your next station before claiming. Audio record into the app.
18. **CURSE OF THE LINGUIST** — Your next claim's challenge must be transacted in German only.
19. **CURSE OF THE PARK** — Your next claim must be within 200m of a park entrance.
20. **CURSE OF THE TWIN** — Visit a station and any other station sharing the same line — but you can only claim one of them. The Hunter picks which.

## POWER CARDS (3, one of each — Runner-only)

### POWER 1: LEAP
> Skip one station. Your next claim may be **2 stops away** on the same line. The intermediate station does **not** join your tail. Still requires completing a Challenge at the destination. Useful for jumping over a difficult section or evading a known Hunter position.

### POWER 2: VEIL
> For the next **60 minutes**, no Skin-Shed ping is sent to the Hunters. Your head station is hidden. The Hunters know only that VEIL is active. After 60 minutes, regular pinging resumes.

### POWER 3: SHED
> Drop the **last 3 stations** from your tail. They re-open for re-entry. You lose the points associated with those 3 stations (both their station-count and their Challenge points). Cannot be used in the first 30 minutes of the game. Use this when you've boxed yourself into a peninsula of your own making.

---

*All cards are public. There are no hidden hands. The Runner knows what curses can be thrown; Hunters know what powers can be used. Play with information, not surprise.*
